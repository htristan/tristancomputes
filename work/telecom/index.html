Leadership through a crisis

Replacing TextNow’s only voice provider.

After TextNow’s only voice platform and number provider went bankrupt, the platform faced shutdown. We had a matter of weeks to secure a new solution and move our service. I led the response and set the approach for the migration.

My role

Migration and negotiation leadership

Outcome

Calling service moved to a new platform before the old one shut down.

In this story

  1. The business was at risk
  2. Negotiate and deliver a way forward
  3. Keep a way back during the cutover
  4. Get the service onto a new platform

The business was at risk

More than a million phone numbers and customer voicemail depended on that platform. Without a replacement, we risked losing the core calling service the business depended on.

Negotiate and deliver a way forward

I negotiated a workable transition and brought carriers and delivery partners together around a replacement solution. I reconciled the number inventory and coordinated capacity for the move. Engineers and delivery partners built the new platform.

Keep a way back during the cutover

I required a carrier-by-carrier cutover, keeping both the old and new voice connections live so we could roll back if something went wrong.

I pushed for smaller migration batches against the vendor’s proposed schedule. With our core calling service at stake, I chose a staged move with a way to recover at each step.

Get the service onto a new platform

We moved calling service before the old platform closed and recovered the customer voicemail archive. I managed the transition and worked through cutover issues with our partners. The business had a path forward.
