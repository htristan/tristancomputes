Platform ownership & cost improvement

Bringing TextNow’s voice platform in-house.

I led the product and commercial work to bring TextNow’s calling platform in-house. The result was ownership of the VoIP service layer and more than 30% in voice-platform cost improvements.

My role

Product and commercial lead

Outcome

More than 30% improvement in voice-platform costs. TextNow operates its own VoIP service layer.

In this story

  1. Own the service layer
  2. Recommend insourcing and build support
  3. Lead the program through delivery
  4. Improve the platform economics

Own the service layer

TextNow relied on an outside provider for its calling platform. Bringing the service layer in-house meant working through the business case, technical plan, delivery relationships, and customer transition.

Recommend insourcing and build support

I had to recommend a direction to our CEO: stay with an outside provider or bring the platform in-house. I weighed the cost and control benefits against the complexity of building, migrating, and operating our own service layer.

I recommended insourcing, then built stakeholder buy-in for that choice. It meant taking on the platform responsibilities alongside the opportunity to improve its economics.

Lead the program through delivery

I led the product and commercial program, working with engineering on the plan through phased migration and cutover. Engineers and an external delivery partner built the platform.

Improve the platform economics

TextNow now operates its backend voice service in-house. Insourcing improved voice-platform costs by more than 30%.
