Product strategy & innovation

Product direction and a revenue-generating Labs portfolio.

I defined multi-year product direction and helped focus TextNow’s Labs portfolio on validating new opportunities alongside the core consumer business.

My role

Product strategy and innovation leader

Outcome

Labs revenue experiments and monetization pilots generated more than $1M in incremental top-line revenue in 2025.

In this story

  1. Choose where to invest
  2. Turn experiments into product decisions
  3. Keep the decision clear

Choose where to invest

A scaled consumer business needs to improve the product customers use today while testing what could grow it next. My responsibility was to set a longer-term direction and help focus the Labs operating model around opportunities worth validating.

Turn experiments into product decisions

I led product strategy and innovation work: framing opportunities, evaluating the business case, and using experiments and prototypes to decide what to pursue.

Teams across TextNow delivered the revenue experiments and monetization pilots that generated more than $1M in incremental top-line revenue in 2025. Yello! became another new product from this innovation work, with me as executive sponsor and product lead.

Keep the decision clear

I want each experiment to answer a product question: is there a customer need, can we deliver the experience, and does the business case justify further investment? That keeps exploration useful to the product business.
